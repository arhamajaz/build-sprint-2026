Sprint Journey

From Idea to Pitch in 48 Hours

Learn how you'll take your concept through ideation, validation, prototyping, and jury evaluation.

01

Identify Real Problems

Select pressing challenges from business, tech, campus life, or local communities.

02

Validate Value Proposition

Analyze target audience, market needs, customer willingness, and feasibility.

03

Build Business Model Canvas

Structure key activities, partners, cost structures, and revenue streams.

04

Develop Working Prototype / MVP

Create a live web application, mobile UI mockup, no-code demo, or service model.

05

Pitch to Expert Jury

Deliver a 5-minute presentation followed by Q&A on stage before judges.

06

Win Rewards & Recognition

Trophies, medals, prizes worth ₹5,000, and official certificates for all participants.
